Before you explore

What is this dashboard?

This is a practice dashboard about data-related jobs. It shows how you can use filters and charts to look for patterns in roles, skills, locations and work arrangements.

01

Choose what to look at

Pick a location, job role, skill or experience level. You can also choose a date range.

02

Apply your filters

Select “Apply filters”. The numbers and charts will update to match your choices.

03

Compare and start again

Look across the charts for differences. Choose “Clear” to see all the examples again.

What this example teaches

Useful charts
start with careful questions.

Before trusting a trend, check where the data came from, what it includes and when it was updated. This dashboard makes the steps visible: filter the records, compare a measure, and keep the limitations alongside the chart.
